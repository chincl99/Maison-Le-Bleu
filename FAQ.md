## Reservations

**Q: How do I make a reservation?**
A: Use the online reservation form on the Reservations page at the bottom of our website. Fill in your name, email address, preferred date, time, and party size, then submit. You will receive a confirmation email shortly after.

**Q: How far in advance should I book?**
A: We recommend booking at least 3–5 days in advance for weekday dining and at least 1–2 weeks ahead for weekend evenings and public holidays. For private dining rooms and group bookings of 10 or more, please contact us at least 3 weeks in advance.

**Q: Can I make a same-day reservation?**
A: Same-day reservations are subject to availability. Please call us directly at +65 6888 8888 to check for last-minute openings.

**Q: What is the maximum party size for an online booking?**
A: Online bookings accommodate up to 10 guests. For larger groups or banquet enquiries, please contact our events team at banquets@goldendragonsq.com.

**Q: How will I know my reservation is confirmed?**
A: You will receive a confirmation message on-screen immediately after submitting the form, and a confirmation email will be sent to the address you provided within 15 minutes.

**Q: Can I modify or cancel my reservation?**
A: To modify or cancel, please email reservations@goldendragonsq.com or call +65 6888 8888 at least 24 hours before your scheduled dining time.

**Q: Is there a cancellation or no-show fee?**
A: Reservations cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice or no-shows may incur a S$50 per-person fee charged to the credit card provided at booking.

## Dining Experience

**Q: What type of cuisine does Golden Dragon Pavilion serve?**
A: We serve imperial Chinese fine dining inspired by the royal kitchens of the Qing Dynasty — refined Cantonese and Shandong preparations alongside seasonal tasting menus that draw on centuries of Chinese culinary tradition.

**Q: Do you offer a tasting menu?**
A: Yes. We offer an eight-course Imperial Tasting Menu available at dinner service. A vegetarian variant is available with advance notice. Please indicate your preference when booking.

**Q: Is the menu available to view online?**
A: A curated selection of our signature dishes is featured in the Menu section of our website. The full seasonal menu changes monthly; please contact us for the current menu.

**Q: Can you accommodate dietary restrictions and allergies?**
A: Yes. We cater to a wide range of dietary requirements including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, nut-free, shellfish-free, and halal-friendly options. Please note any allergies or dietary restrictions in the "Special Requests" field when booking, or inform your server upon arrival.

**Q: Do you serve Halal food?**
A: We offer halal-friendly dishes and our kitchen follows strict separation protocols. However, we are not a fully halal-certified restaurant. Guests with strict halal requirements should contact us before visiting so we can advise accordingly.

**Q: Is there a dress code?**
A: Smart casual or above is required. We kindly ask guests not to wear shorts, flip-flops, or athletic wear. For private dining and special occasions, formal attire is encouraged.

## Opening Hours & Location

**Q: What are your opening hours?**
A: We are open Tuesday through Sunday.
- Lunch: 12:00 pm – 2:30 pm (last order 2:00 pm)
- Dinner: 6:00 pm – 10:30 pm (last order 10:00 pm)

We are closed on Mondays. Hours may vary on public holidays; please check our website or call ahead.

**Q: Where are you located?**
A: Golden Dragon Pavilion is located in the heart of Singapore's Civic District. Full address: 1 Golden Dragon Court, Singapore 049213. We are a 5-minute walk from City Hall MRT (Exit B).

**Q: Is parking available?**
A: Valet parking is available from 6:00 pm on weekdays and from 12:00 pm on weekends (S$12 flat rate). Public parking is available at adjacent carparks.

## Private Dining & Events

**Q: Do you have private dining rooms?**
A: Yes. We have three private dining rooms: the Jade Chamber (seats 8–12), the Phoenix Hall (seats 12–20), and the Imperial Banquet Suite (seats 20–60). All rooms feature dedicated service staff and can be configured for business dinners, wedding banquets, and celebratory events.

**Q: Can you host corporate events and celebrations?**
A: Absolutely. Our events team can tailor a full-service package including bespoke menus, floral arrangements, audio-visual support, and personalised keepsakes. Email banquets@goldendragonsq.com to begin planning.

**Q: Do you offer set menus for group bookings?**
A: Yes. Group set menus start from S$128 per person for parties of 8 or more. Customised menus are available with 2 weeks' notice.

## Children & Accessibility

**Q: Are children welcome?**
A: Children aged 8 and above are welcome. In respect of our other diners, we ask that children be accompanied and supervised at all times. High chairs are available upon request.

**Q: Is the restaurant wheelchair accessible?**
A: Yes. Our premises are fully wheelchair accessible, including the entrance, all dining areas, and restrooms. Please let us know when booking if you require any specific accommodations.

## Payments & Gift Vouchers

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayNow, and cash. We do not accept cheques.

**Q: Do you offer gift vouchers?**
A: Yes. Gift vouchers in denominations of S$50, S$100, S$200, and S$500 are available for purchase at the restaurant or by contacting us at info@goldendragonsq.com.

**Q: Is service charge included in the bill?**
