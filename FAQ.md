# FAQ — 金龍閣 · Golden Dragon Pavilion

Frequently asked questions for use as a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) knowledge base.

## Reservations

**Q: How do I make a reservation?**
A: Use the online reservation form in the Reservations section at the bottom of our website. Fill in your name, email address, preferred date, time, and party size, then submit. A confirmation will appear on-screen immediately and be sent to your email.

**Q: How far in advance should I book?**
A: We recommend at least 3–5 days ahead for weekday dining and 1–2 weeks for weekend evenings and public holidays. For private dining rooms and group bookings of 10 or more, please contact us at least 3 weeks in advance.

**Q: Can I make a same-day reservation?**
A: Same-day reservations are subject to availability. Please call us directly at +65 6234 5678 to check for last-minute openings.

**Q: What is the maximum party size for an online booking?**
A: Online bookings accommodate up to 12 guests. For larger groups or banquet enquiries, please contact our events team at banquets@goldendragonpavilion.sg.

**Q: Can I modify or cancel my reservation?**
A: Email reservations@goldendragonpavilion.sg or call +65 6234 5678 at least 24 hours before your scheduled dining time to modify or cancel.

**Q: Is there a cancellation or no-show fee?**
A: Reservations cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice or no-shows may incur a S$50 per-person fee.

## Dining Experience

**Q: What type of cuisine does Golden Dragon Pavilion serve?**
A: We serve imperial Chinese fine dining inspired by the royal kitchens of the Qing Dynasty — refined Cantonese and Shandong preparations alongside seasonal tasting menus that draw on centuries of Chinese culinary tradition.

**Q: Do you offer a tasting menu?**
A: Yes. We offer an eight-course Imperial Tasting Menu available at dinner service. A vegetarian variant is available with advance notice. Please indicate your preference when booking.

**Q: Is the menu available to view online?**
A: A curated selection of our signature dishes is featured in the Menu section of this website. The full seasonal menu changes monthly; please contact us for the current menu.

**Q: Can you accommodate dietary restrictions and allergies?**
A: Yes. We cater to vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, nut-free, shellfish-free, and halal-friendly requirements. Note any needs in the Special Requests field when booking, or inform your server upon arrival.

**Q: Do you serve Halal food?**
A: We offer halal-friendly dishes and our kitchen follows strict separation protocols. However, we are not a fully halal-certified restaurant. Guests with strict halal requirements should contact us before visiting so we can advise accordingly.

**Q: Is there a dress code?**
A: Smart casual or above is required. Shorts, flip-flops, and athletic wear are not permitted. Formal attire is encouraged for private dining and special occasions.

## Private Dining & Events

**Q: Do you have private dining rooms?**
A: Yes. We have three private dining rooms: the Jade Chamber (8–12 pax), the Phoenix Hall (12–20 pax), and the Imperial Banquet Suite (20–60 pax). All rooms feature dedicated service staff and can be configured for business dinners, wedding banquets, and celebratory events.

**Q: Can you host corporate events and celebrations?**
A: Absolutely. Our events team can tailor a full-service package including bespoke menus, floral arrangements, audio-visual support, and personalised keepsakes. Email banquets@goldendragonpavilion.sg to begin planning.

**Q: Do you offer set menus for group bookings?**
A: Yes. Group set menus start from S$128 per person for parties of 8 or more. Customised menus are available with 2 weeks' notice.

## Children & Accessibility

**Q: Are children welcome?**
A: Children aged 8 and above are welcome. We ask that children be accompanied and supervised at all times. High chairs are available upon request.

**Q: Is the restaurant wheelchair accessible?**
A: Yes. Our premises are fully wheelchair accessible, including the entrance, all dining areas, and restrooms. Please let us know when booking if you require any specific accommodations.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
